Competition robotics

On my team, I work on the robot from the first sketch through competition. That includes designing mechanisms, writing autonomous code, testing, fixing problems, and helping with match strategy.

One change to the robot usually affects several other things. A new mechanism can change the code, the way it drives, and the match plan, so I try to test the whole robot together instead of treating each part separately.

Engineering notebooks

I have written more than 200 pages across two engineering notebooks. They include sketches, test results, failed ideas, code notes, design changes, and competition analysis.

I use the notebook to keep track of why we made each decision, what happened during testing, and what we changed afterward. It also helps us avoid repeating the same mistakes later.

Both notebooks follow the robots from early ideas and rough sketches to finished builds and competition results.
